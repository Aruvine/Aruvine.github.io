Description of Service

Wind Bot is a Discord bot providing the following features:

  • Auto-Wave Partner System: automated exchange of server advertisements between participating Discord servers.
  • Partner Link Tracking: recording and displaying server partnership statistics.
  • DonutSMP Spawner Shop: a ticket-based system for buying and selling in-game Minecraft spawners on the DonutSMP server, including stock tracking, staff payouts, sales statistics, a priority queue, and staff applications.

Building Service

The building service lets you order an in-game build from a builder on the DonutSMP building team. Unlike a spawner trade, a build is paid for up front and the payment is held until the build is done. By ordering a build, you acknowledge and agree that:

  • You pay the agreed price in-game to the shop owner, or to the owner's appointed stand-in while the owner is away. Wind Bot itself never holds or moves any currency; it records the order and checks that the payment arrived.
  • To check a payment, the bot reads both players' in-game balances from DonutSMP. You may be asked to pay an exact, slightly unusual amount so the payment can be recognised.
  • Once you accept the finished build, the payment continues to be held for 48 hours. During that time you can report a problem, such as the build being griefed.
  • If you report a problem, the owner or their stand-in decides whether to refund you or to pay the builder. That decision is final.
  • After the 48 hours pass without a report, the builder is paid their share and the order cannot be disputed through the bot.
  • The Developer, the owner, and builders are not liable for any loss of in-game currency, items, or builds beyond what is described above.
